My Story

Built companies. Hit real
problems. Built the solution.

The Builder

I grew up in it.

I grew up in my dad's house, inundated with startups. Company building was dinner table conversation. I took his entrepreneurship classes at BYU, then spent a summer helping run an accelerator, where I found the problem I'd end up building around. I never really wondered what direction I was headed. The only question was what I'd build first.

The Operator

Started from scratch.

Not a theorizer, not an advisor. Someone who shipped products, hacked growth, and figured out how to make money. Six companies. Three exits. Including DevMountain, which I co-founded off a $9 domain name and grew into one of the most respected code schools in the country before it was acquired in year four.

The Realization

I saw the same patterns everywhere.

Founders building before validating. Chasing venture dollars instead of revenue. Scaling prematurely. Dying quietly by wasting time, effort, and resources. I realized I know more than I thought.

The System

So I built Startup Ignition.

My dad and I teamed up after our exits to teach what we'd figured out: validate before you build, and build businesses that actually work. We started with a bootcamp to pressure-test ideas and teach the fundamentals. Now, with over 1,000 ventures having gone through it, we've seen consistent success from disciplined founders who apply these principles early.

The Educator

Teaching became a multiplier.

Today, I also teach at universities like BYU (CS 405) and UVU (CS 4960R), helping technical founders become better entrepreneurs. As the bootcamp grew and spread across the country, we expanded the Startup Ignition ecosystem, adding tools that force validation, and eventually, a venture fund.

The Fund

Enter Startup Ignition Ventures.

The capital extension of that system. For the past 7 years, my dad and I have been angel investing together, backing disciplined founders who follow the same methodologies, and seeing consistent success. As more people took notice, it evolved into something bigger. Today, as a VC fund, we invest in early-stage founders focused on building real businesses: revenue over hype, validation over assumptions, discipline over wishing. We don't chase hype narratives. We back companies that become elephants: profitable, durable, and built to last.

What I Believe

Tyler's Startup Ignition
Philosophy

Most startup advice is noise. Here's what actually matters.

« »

Ideas Are Hypotheses

Your startup idea is not precious. It's an untested hypothesis. Treat it like one. Validate relentlessly before you build.

$

Revenue Is Truth

Pitch decks lie. Projections lie. Revenue doesn't. The fastest path to knowing if your business works is getting someone to pay you.

↑

Validation > Building

Stop building features nobody asked for. Talk to customers. Run experiments. The graveyard is full of beautiful products nobody wanted.

∞

Elephants > Unicorns

We don't chase billion-dollar fantasies. We back founders building real, revenue-generating businesses with strong unit economics. Elephants, not unicorns.

×

Premature Scaling Kills

Hiring before product-market fit. Spending before revenue. Raising before traction. The #1 startup killer isn't bad ideas. It's bad timing.

→

Timing > Capital

Money doesn't fix broken businesses. Timing, validation, and execution do. Capital is fuel, but only after the engine works.
